## 10. RESEARCH STATUS

The implementation **does not support the paper as written**:

1. Different embedder (Y-luminance vs RGB-averaged LL + scaling map).
2. Different watermark derivation (counter-prefixed SHA-256).
3. Different split (700/100/100), stack (PyTorch), and CNN details (BatchNorm, one dropout).
4. The paper's central table (Fig. 6) — 18 blind-accuracy points — cannot be generated: there is one blind checkpoint, at an α the paper never uses, and its accuracy (0.78) is below every 64-bit entry in the table except α=0.005 (0.73).
5. PSNR/SSIM trends in Fig. 3 (PSNR falling with bit length) contradict the repo, where PSNR is flat in bit length.
6. The paper omits the registry-ID / repetition layer that makes the repo's demo work.

What the repo *does* support: DWT-SVD multiplicative embedding with ~46–51 dB PSNR, a genuinely blind 1D-CNN decoder with a 15-value window, and a non-blind reference.

## 12. FINAL VERDICT

**A. What have we actually achieved?**
A correct, tested DWT-SVD multiplicative embedder (Y-channel, haar, LL) with 46–51 dB PSNR; a non-blind reference decoder; a **genuinely blind** windowed 1D-CNN decoder trained once on DIV2K (64-bit, α=0.02) reaching ~0.78 bit accuracy and 96 % exact registry-ID recovery thanks to 15× repetition; an attack library; a working FastAPI + web demo (with an env-var caveat); 372 passing unit tests.

**B. What remains?**
Everything the paper's results section depends on: blind decoders at the paper's α and widths, the executed 18-point matrix with committed results and figures, robustness runs with the current decoder, the paper's actual RGB embedder (or a paper rewrite), dataset reproducibility, and README/metrics consistency.

**C. What prevents the project from being considered complete?**
No committed experimental results at all; one blind checkpoint at an off-paper operating point; default demo path broken (503); training/test payload leakage; paper and code describe different algorithms.

**D. Single most important next task?**
Task 3 + 4 together: retrain the windowed decoder on random payloads across the paper's α × bit-length grid and record results automatically — without that, the paper's central table has no support.

**E. What experiments are still missing?**

## What the measurements say about the paper draft

* **Blind accuracy is 0.48–0.76**, never above 0.77, at every point of the grid. The paper's Fig. 6 (1.00 at 8 bits, ≥0.81 everywhere) is not reproducible with this codebase.
* **The trend is inverted**: short payloads are the *hardest* (8-bit: 0.48–0.61) because bits 0–7 sit on the steep head of the singular-value spectrum; per-position accuracy climbs from ~0.6 (bits 0–7) to ~0.8 (bits 32–63).
* **PSNR is flat in payload length** (49.0 → 40.5 dB is driven by α alone), unlike the paper's Fig. 3.
* **Robustness**: only JPEG ≥ q90 and noise σ ≤ 2 keep blind BER near the clean value (0.25); every low-pass or geometric attack collapses both decoders to ~0.45–0.5.
* Non-blind recovery (needs the original) is 0.99 at 8–16 bits and 0.83–0.87 at 128–256 bits.
